# Telco-communication-customer-churn
This Project is a Telecommunications Customer Churn data. It contains customer demographics, account information, internet and phone services, payment methods, monthly charges, total charges. My analyses answers some key business questions that a telecom business would genuinely care about. 

# Key Business questions my analysis answered 
What percentage of customers have churned?
Which contract type has the highest churn rate?
Are customers with shorter tenure more likely to leave?
Does churn vary by internet service type?
Which payment methods are associated with higher churn?
Are customers paying higher monthly charges more likely to churn?
Does having services such as Online Security or Tech Support relate to lower churn?
Which customer segments contribute the most monthly charges that are at risk due to churn?
Does churn differ between senior and non-senior customers or customers with and without dependents?
